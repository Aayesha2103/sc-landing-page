The Student's Journey

STEP 01

Face ID Login

Students can securely access their SnapClass account using Face ID. Simply position your face in the camera frame and let the AI identify you instantly.

STEP 02

Quick Profile Registration

If SnapClass does not recognize a student, they can quickly create a profile by entering their name and enrolling their voice for voice-based attendance.

STEP 03

Join Your Classes

Students can join their classes using the subject code shared by their teacher. Enter the code, enroll instantly, and access the course from your dashboard.

STEP 04

Track Your Classes

View all your enrolled subjects, subject codes, sections, and attendance statistics from one simple dashboard. Students can also manage their course enrollment whenever needed.
